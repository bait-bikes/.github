# Community conduct

Bait Bikes contributors must communicate professionally, respect lived experience, and avoid demeaning language based on identity, transportation choice, housing status, criminal history, or institutional role.

Project discussions should be evidence-led and must not promote vigilantism, discriminatory targeting, unsafe confrontation, indiscriminate surveillance, or public identification of uncharged people. Critique ideas and systems directly; protect the dignity and privacy of people.

Organization maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct undermines a safe, productive community.
