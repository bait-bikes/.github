# Bait Bikes

**Mobility is worth protecting.**

Bait Bikes is a public-interest technology and education initiative helping law enforcement, cities, counties, states, transit systems, campuses, bike shops, manufacturers, and riders respond to bicycle and e-bike theft with better prevention, trustworthy evidence, safer operations, and cross-jurisdiction coordination.

Our work focuses on:

- responsible, policy-led bait-bike operations;
- bicycle-theft pattern and fencing intelligence;
- secure built-in tracking for bicycles and e-bikes;
- owner-controlled mobile recovery workflows;
- shared contracts across Rust, Dart, and TypeScript systems; and
- privacy, civil liberties, safe response, and public accountability.

Learn more at [bait-bikes.github.io](https://bait-bikes.github.io).

> Bicycle theft can disrupt work, care, education, family life, and public service. High-value bikes may also be part of organized interstate or international resale. We take the property, the mobility, and the people seriously.
